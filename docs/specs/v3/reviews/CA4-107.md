## Decisions (developer-approved)

1. Closed list of four sources, shown in House and Devices.
2. On-demand slow-path computation; nothing stored but dismissals.
3. Accept = prefilled add form, every undetermined meaning mandatory; never applied without apply.
4. Multiple selection only for rooms from areas.
5. Dismissal installation-wide, restorable, dropped with its source.
