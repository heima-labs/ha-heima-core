# CA4-124 — audiences, the house audience and a content-free fallback

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-19).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Impact record

- **Existing decisions.** Approved people model §8, Q3, Q4, Q8, Q11; CA4-118 recipients anchored to
  Persons; CA4-121 `household.guest_stay`; Layer 6 kinds, audiences, digests, delivery, fallback,
  rendering, generation; Layer 5 advisory and action-notice occurrences; Layer 8 §9.14 map card.
- **Gap.** One audience per kind; tablets and guests received nothing; the fallback showed resident
  content to every HA user, guests and device accounts included; administrator kinds were not
  checked for resident personal data although the administrator may be an installer.
- **Overlap check.** No `guests` audience (Q4): `house` covers devices, the card banner and guests
  during a stay. `HouseEndpoint` is a minimal endpoint record, not a person recipient. The descriptor
  flag `on_site_actionable` follows the existing `resident_action_notice` pattern; the occurrence
  carries it so Layer 6 never reads the Layer-5 registry; reading `household.guest_stay` follows the
  existing `quiet_hours.active` precedent.
- **Owners.** Layer 1 §3.1; Layer 5 §3.2 descriptor contract, §6.0 occurrences and on-site delivery,
  §8; Layer 6 §4.1, §4.2, §5.1, §5.2, §5.3, §6.4, §7.2, §7.3, §7.8, §8.1, §10, §14; Layer 8 §9.14,
  §13, §16; overview.
- **Out of scope, recorded in the backlog.** Incongruence warnings (alarm armed away while occupied,
  door unlocked or windows open while empty) need their own kinds and a security family.

## Decisions (developer-approved 2026-10-19)

1. `NotificationKindDescriptor.audiences` is a set; `house` is admissible only for
   `reaction.advisory` and `reaction.action_notice`, always with `residents`.
2. Descriptor field `on_site_actionable`, copied as `house_delivery` into occurrences: `True` for
   external exchange and solar shading advice and weather-protection notices.
3. `house` = `HouseEndpoint`s, guest recipients while any guest stay is active, and the map-card
   banner; never answer buttons; never digested; household quiet hours apply; an empty `house` never
   triggers the fallback.
4. The fallback `persistent_notification` carries no content, only the audience and panel link.
5. `administrators` kinds carry no resident personal data.
6. A notice text never addresses the reader as the person who acted.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| "Close the windows" advisory during a stay | Residents, tablet endpoint (no buttons), guest's endpoints, card banner. |
| Same advisory without a stay | Residents, tablet, banner; the guest is not reached. |
| Weather-protection notice, no tablet configured | Residents and banner; no fallback. |
| Recovery confirmation | Residents only; never `house`. |
| Resident recipient unreachable | Content-free `persistent_notification` with the panel link. |
| Guest opens the fallback link | The panel shows nothing without a role. |
| Advisory at night | Tablet follows household quiet hours; no `house` digest. |
| Guest closes the banner | Hidden on that card only; no feedback to Heima. |
