# CA4-20 — cycle instant, job basis and clock discontinuity

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-10).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Approved decisions and exact gap

The developer approved three linked rules: every `AtJob` declares an `elapsed` or `wall_clock`
basis, with wall-clock jobs re-armed after a clock discontinuity greater than 1 second (20a/A);
every cycle receives `max(wall_clock_reading, previous_cycle_instant + 1 µs)` (20b/A); and a
discontinuity is declared as a new `clock_discontinuity` observation-gap reason (20c/A). Relaxing
the presence adapter to `<=` was considered and rejected: it would leave equal-time groups that
Layer 3 merges into one concurrent observation, colliding episode keys, and no answer to a
backward clock.

Layer 0 §3.1 claimed a monotonic countdown cannot fire early or late even for absolute
deadlines; §4.1 allowed distinct cycles with equal `when`; Layer 4 §9.3 required a strict
inequality those cycles could violate; and no rule covered wall-clock regression or forward jumps
in recorded history.

## Reuse and owners

Layer 0 §3.1 owns job basis and re-arm through the existing `job_id` replacement. The new §3.5
owns the cycle instant and discontinuity detection, reusing the existing 60-second continuity
recurrence and the durable observation-continuity metadata path of §4.4. `ObservationGapReason`
gains `clock_discontinuity`. Layer 2 (source-age and dwell jobs), Layer 5 (next-step and delayed
slots) declare their basis; Layer 3 §4.2/§4.4.2 and Layer 4 §9.3 consume the strict ordering and
the new reason. Overview and recap summarize. No new scheduler, clock service or per-channel journal.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Required result |
|---|---|
| Two cycles read the same microsecond | Instants `T` and `T + 1 µs`; presence departure satisfies the strict adapter rule. |
| Ingress edge stamped before the previous cycle instant | Raised to `previous + 1 µs`, ingress order kept. |
| 0.5 s wall correction | Diagnostic only; no gap, no re-arm. |
| 2 s backward correction | Instants keep increasing by 1 µs; `clock_regression`; gap until wall clock passes the floor; wall-clock jobs re-armed. |
| 10 min forward NTP jump (RTC-less boot) | Gap over the skipped interval; a calendar job due inside it fires once immediately. |
| 30-minute fixed hold during a correction | `elapsed` job expires after 30 monotonic minutes, unchanged. |
| Restart with wall clock 1 h behind the durable floor | Backward discontinuity at bootstrap; history does not move backward. |
| Room-return survival or correction-count window across a gap | Censored/excluded as an observation gap, never a measured duration. |

This is document-level evidence; no V3 runtime test has run.
