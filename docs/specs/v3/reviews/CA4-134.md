# CA4-134 — family of posture-check advisories

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-21).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Impact record

- **Existing decisions.** CA4-126: posture-check advisories are `safety` (immediate, quiet hours
  included, residents only). Layer 6 §6.1 namespace-to-family table for follow-up, availability and
  action notices; Layer 6 §6.4 advisory digest; `PersonalFamily` excludes `safety`.
- **Gap.** Layer 6 §4.2 fixed `reaction.advisory` to `suggestion`, and its occurrence had no
  descriptor id (audit F-L5b-03, F-L6-01).
- **Overlap check.** Reuses the namespace mechanism and the `reaction_descriptor_id` field the other
  occurrences already carry; a separate advisory table keeps non-safety advisories `suggestion`
  instead of the `attention` mapping of the other kinds. No new kind.
- **Owners.** Layer 6 §4.2, §6.1, §6.4, §14; Layer 5 §6.0 and §8.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Door unlocked, house empty, 03:00, quiet hours | `safety` advisory delivered at once with buttons; not digested. |
