# CA4-120 — casa occupancy and household presence

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-19).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Impact record

- **Existing decisions.** Approved people model §3.4, §5, invariant 8, D2, D7, Q2, Q10; CA4-118
  `Person` registry; Layer 2 evidence namespaces, admission and `physical_evidence.weighted_evidence`;
  Layer 4 §9.2–§9.3 casa `presence` claim and its one transition adapter; Layer 5 guard capability
  `presence` and `planned_absence_program`; Layer 3 thermal absence cohorts; Layer 8 entities.
- **Gap.** One casa claim `presence`, fused from any evidence including trackers, meant "someone
  inside" or "the household is home" depending on the administrator's bindings. With a guest or a
  house sitter alone, absence programs, routines and thermal learning could not tell the two apart.
- **Overlap check.** Casa occupancy reuses the room `occupancy` fact family and the existing weighted
  evidence formula; household presence reuses `WeightedQuorumStrategy`, `Stability` and the
  `StateTransitionEpisodeAdapter`. The only new producers are core-derived from `Person`; no new
  strategy, formula or episode mechanism.
- **Owners.** Layer 1 §3 taxonomy example; Layer 2 §2.4, §2.8.1, §3, §3.x evidence paragraphs,
  §5.3, §9; Layer 3 §4.4.4 example, §4.8 comfort profile, §10/§11 thermal cohorts; Layer 4 §3.1,
  §6, §9, §9.2, §9.3, §10; Layer 5 §3.2 guard inventory and descriptor guards, `planned_absence_program`
  rule 2, §7.2, §8; Layer 8 §9.1 header, §10.1 entities; recap.
- **Deferred.** Declared household context (`household.planned_absence`,
  `household.guest_stay`) in CA4-121; pause policy and minimum absence duration in CA4-122; learning
  exclusions in CA4-123.

## Decisions (developer-approved 2026-10-19)

1. Casa occupancy is `occupancy@casa` (`present | absent`) from physical evidence only; identity
   trackers cannot be casa occupancy Sensors.
2. Every classified Person's HA person is core-derived `corroborating` casa occupancy evidence
   (resident or guest), never sufficient alone without a confirmation dwell.
3. Household presence is `presence@casa` (`home | away`) from resident Persons' HA persons via a
   core-generated `presence.household` fusion; untracked residents are excluded with a diagnostic.
4. Two transition adapters: `occupancy.departure/return` and `presence.departure/return`.
5. Guard capability `presence` becomes `occupancy`; absence programs read household presence; thermal
   absence cohorts use occupancy episodes.
6. Two read-only HA entities; the map header shows occupancy to all and household presence to
   residents.

**Values set in this pass (to confirm):** household fusion `min_available_weight` = number of tracked
residents (a temporarily unavailable resident makes presence unavailable, never `away`); default
`Stability(to_true=120 s, to_false=600 s, unavailable_grace=300 s)`, editable by the administrator.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Last resident leaves, guest stays inside | `presence.departure`; occupancy stays `present`, no occupancy episode. |
| Guest leaves later, house empty | `occupancy.departure`. |
| Resident phone home, resident asleep, PIR quiet | Person evidence corroborates; with the rule's true dwell, occupancy stays `present`. |
| Administrator binds a `device_tracker` as casa occupancy evidence | Rejected at staging. |
| One resident `not_home`, the other's tracker unavailable | `presence` unavailable after the grace; absence programs undecidable, not started. |
| Untracked child alone at home | Diagnostic; `presence=away`, `occupancy=present` (occupied without residents). |
| Routine "projector on → dim lights" with a guest alone | Guard `occupancy=present` passes; routine acts. |
| Presence simulation during a vacation with a sitter inside | Guard requires `occupancy=absent`; it does not act. |
| Planned absence with a cleaner arriving | Household stays `away`; no `presence.return`; program not ended by the cleaner. |
| Thermal learning during a sitter's stay | Not an absence cohort (occupancy `present`). |
