# CA4-119 — manual control: how separated from who

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-19).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Impact record

- **Existing decisions.** Approved people model D6, invariant 4 and §6 (holds for everyone);
  CA4-118 `UserActor` and `actor_type="physical"`; Layer 0 §5.1.1 control edge, §5.1.1.1 channel
  state, §6.1 hold origin; Layer 5 §6.1 correction attribution; Layer 3 §4.4.2 correction count and
  §4.4.3 routine learner; Layer 8 §8 explanations and §9.8 map control.
- **Gap.** `actuator.resident_control` existed only for residents, so a guest's or tablet's control
  left no record of who acted; `HoldOrigin` values `resident_manual`/`admin_manual` mixed how with
  who, and a resident who is also an administrator fell out of learning as `admin_manual`.
- **Overlap check.** No new mechanism: the same edge, ingress, join and hold. `ControlActor` is a
  closed projection of `UserActor` plus `physical`, not a second actor model. The event keeps its
  three-field value; who acted is provenance. `resident_bundle`/`no_resident_control` stay: they
  name resident evidence exactly.
- **Owners.** Layer 0 §4 claim, §5 `ControlActor`, §5.1.1, §5.1.1.1, §6.1, §13; Layer 1 §3.1;
  Layer 2 §6.1 wording; Layer 3 §4.4.2, §4.4.3, §11; Layer 5 §6.0.1 wording, §6.1, §8; Layer 8 §8,
  §9.8; overview, recap, CA4-01 serialization examples.
- **Deferred.** Excluding periods "occupied without residents" and guest rooms during a stay from
  learning (CA4-123); public text for viewers without a role (CA4-125).

## Decisions (developer-approved 2026-10-19)

1. `ControlActor = resident | guest | device | administrator | physical`, derived from `UserActor`
   (resident wins over the administrator flag) or the approved physical adapter.
2. `actuator.manual_control` replaces `actuator.resident_control`, emitted for every verified manual
   control; owner `layer0.manual_control_ingress`.
3. `HoldOrigin` and channel-state origin `manual` replace `resident_manual`/`admin_manual`;
   `HoldLifecycleValue.manual_actor`.
4. `CorrectionAttributionEvent.classification="manual_override"` with `control_actor`.
5. Resident learning evidence is `resident` or `physical` only; `guest`, `device`, `administrator`
   are confounders that exclude a window, never negatives.
6. Explanations show the origin and, to residents and administrators, the coarse `manual_actor`.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Guest turns off from the tablet a light a Reaction keeps on | Hold `origin="manual"`, `manual_actor="guest"`; `manual_override` with `control_actor="guest"`; no learning evidence. |
| Resident who is an HA administrator changes a setpoint | `manual_control` with `ControlActor="resident"`; valid evidence. |
| Installer moves a cover during a routine assembly window | `administrator`: not a member; window ambiguous and excluded for candidates requiring that channel. |
| Approved wall-switch adapter turns a light on | `physical`: valid member (subject to CA4-123 exclusions). |
| Tablet user with no Person dims a lamp | `device`: hold applies; never counted. |
| HA automation changes the light | `external_automation`; no `manual_control`; diagnostic correction only. |
| Dumb wall switch cuts power | Unavailable transition; no event, no hold (unchanged). |
| Serialization examples | `origin="manual"`, `manual_actor="resident"`; recomputed sizes 1844/1306/1362 bytes, depth 2. |
