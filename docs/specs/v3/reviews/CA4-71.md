# CA4-71 — closing phases and undelivered retirement for grouped notifications

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-12).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Decision (approved with C3/C4)

- Layer 0 §4.5: `ready -> retired` without delivery when Layer 6 decides no notice is due; the
  audit fact committed with `ready` remains. One delivery may cover several ready records. No new
  outbox field: the delivery channel is Layer-6 diagnostics.
- Layer 5 `ProposalReviewOccurrence`: new `closed` phase (`accepted`, `rejected`,
  `proposal_incompatible`, `not_approvable`) and `cause_record_id`, which keys the operation id.
- Layer 5 `ReactionAdvisoryOccurrence`: `opened`/`closed` phases (`activation_ended`,
  `authorization_disabled`, `superseded`). Closing phases are never delivered to people.

## Overlap check

No new fact type, store, outbox phase or schedule. `cause_record_id` points to existing §4.1
records (initial evidence revision, readiness assessment, review transition). The closing phases
follow the pattern already used by `ConsentRequestOccurrence` and `RecoveryConfirmationOccurrence`.
The B1 rule "no resident notice that advice is no longer needed" is preserved.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Three proposals created in 30 min, one approved from the panel before the window ends | One digest counting two; the approved one's record retires undelivered. |
| Proposal approvable, not approvable, approvable again under one evidence revision | `created`, `closed(not_approvable)`, `approvable_again` with distinct operation ids. |
| Every open proposal is closed after a digest was delivered | Layer 6 clears the digest on the phone through its tag. |
| Advisory opened at 03:00 in quiet hours, activation still open at 07:00 | Delivered in the end-of-quiet-hours digest. |
| Advisory opened at 03:00, activation ended at 05:00 | `closed` fact appended; opening record retires undelivered; no resident notice. |
| Restart with an undelivered pre-restart advisory | Treated as closed: retired undelivered, no fact appended. |
| Retry of the same closing phase | Same operation id; one fact, one record. |

Textual review only; no V3 runtime test has run.
