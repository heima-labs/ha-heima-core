## Managed episode restart impact record (approved, partial)

**Decision.** A descriptor that explicitly permits resuming its own actuator episode may act after restart only with durable proof that Heima physically started that episode, fresh current channel state and current Reaction authority. An active actuator without that proof is left untouched and the resident is notified. The proof is a compact current-state projection, written on confirmed opening and closure in Layer 0's ordinary end-of-cycle batch; it is not a synchronous per-command journal. A crash before the opening batch commits leaves no proof. A restart restores no hold, timer, missed step or historical learning interval.

**Gap and reuse.** Layer 5 §6.1's `complete_observation` can be produced by another actor and its event history is prunable. Layer 0 §5.1.1.1 retains a current physical projection but no durable managed-episode authority. §7.4's checkpoint covers accepted power-recovery targets, not confirmed ordinary ownership. Reuse Layer 0 state-fact write-on-change, latest-record retention, cycle batching and fresh restart observation. Layer 5 owns the descriptor opt-in and the causal opening/closure; Layer 0 owns persistence and lookup. Layer 6 owns resident delivery through the availability path specified below. The overview, recap, backlog and registry depend on this slice.

**V2-to-V3 constraints and counterexamples.** Keep one generic Reaction/ApplyStep path and descriptor-declared semantics; no fan-specific lease or HA helper. A fan active before a qualifying extraction start is never claimed, even at a matching speed. A previously inactive fan that Heima physically turns on can be resumed only after a committed opening and fresh active reading. A matching target produced by an external automation does not open the marker. A fresh off reading, ended program, changed generation or revoked consent prevents reuse. A crash before opening commit or an invalid marker leaves active hardware untouched; a resident notice follows at every restart once a fresh active reading is available, regardless of the action trigger. An unavailable channel cannot be commanded or declared safely managed. No V3 runtime test has run; the full extraction speed/stop program remains open CA4-12 work.

| Restart scenario | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Confirmed Heima start, committed open state, fresh active reading, authorized ongoing program | New activation evaluates current target and ordinary gates; no missed step replay. |
| Speed changes while episode remains open | Physical channel history changes; managed-episode state does not write again. |
| Physical start succeeds, crash precedes opening commit | No durable proof; current active channel is left untouched and a new resident notice is requested after the first fresh active post-restart reading. |
| External automation reaches the same target or fan was active at entry | No Heima-start projection; no ownership claim or later stop. |
| Normal end, generation replacement or authority revocation | Closed projection; restart cannot resume the old episode. |
| Power/HA gap, then fresh unavailable or inactive channel | No command while unavailable; inactive state closes any open marker and enters ordinary start evaluation. Historical learning interval stays incomplete. |

**Remaining CA4-12 work.** The earlier one-shot extraction draft had `managed_episode_resumption=False`. Initial-V3 extraction now requires a registered ongoing start/change/end specialization with `managed_episode_resumption=True` and fixed-duration hold release. Its concrete program implementation must be specified before the resumption and notice behavior can be staged. The approved persistence and availability rules do not claim that program already exists.

## Unmanaged active channel notice impact record (approved)

**Decision and exact gap.** Use the existing Layer-3 `AutomationSuppressionReason` and `AvailabilitySuppressionOccurrence` contract, with Layer 5 as owner and Layer 6 as the sole renderer/delivery owner. The new reason applies at every restart to a current automatically authorized descriptor with `managed_episode_resumption=True`, when the first fresh valid channel reading is active and no usable committed managed-episode proof exists. It is independent of the action trigger and of `ReactionEvaluationResult`, which may correctly be `not_triggered`. The notification says Heima is leaving the active actuator alone and will not assume responsibility for a later automatic stop; it cannot assign the action to a resident.

**Reuse and affected contracts.** Layer 3 §6.3.1 owns the closed reason vocabulary and transient episode tracker contract; Layer 5 §3.2/§6–7 owns the exact detection and suppression; Layer 6 reuses its existing `automation.availability` mapping. The reason's episode remains active while the channel remains actively unmanaged, even if the initiating sensor later falls below its trigger, so a `restored` transition cannot falsely announce that the device turned off. Layer 0's managed-episode state and channel-state reading remain the only restart evidence. The overview, recap, backlog, registry and acceptance scenarios depend on this slice. No new fact type, synchronous follow-up preparation, HA helper, per-cycle notification or V2 behavior is introduced.

**Cross-kind counterexamples.** A previously active fan after a normal in-process qualifying start is a descriptor no-op, not this restart warning. The same post-restart active/no-proof condition may apply to another future ongoing descriptor on a cover, valve or light channel if it declares the capability and a meaningful active state. A valid open marker, fresh inactive channel, unreadable channel, disabled Reaction or revoked consent does not satisfy the reason. Falling humidity without a physical off edge must not emit a misleading recovery message. A later fresh inactive reading ends the suppression without claiming Heima switched the device off. This is textual review, not an executable V3 test.

**Delivery edge resolved.** The developer chose an availability notice on
every process restart when an automatically authorized resumable Reaction's
channel is freshly observed active without usable managed-episode proof,
regardless of whether its start trigger currently qualifies. This supersedes
the earlier trigger-gated proposal, which could lose a warning if

the notification key for each process run so an earlier delivery cannot

is reused; no synchronous preparation or separate durable projection is
needed. This is a current-state warning, never evidence that a
resident acted or that an interrupted learning window was complete.

### Extraction fan already in use (approved product direction, contract still open)

**Correction after developer clarification.** The earlier wording was too broad
when it implied that an extraction Reaction may never vary fan speed. The
decision is made at the qualifying start of one intervention: an already
active fan is outside that intervention's actuation, whatever its observed
speed or attributed origin; an initially inactive fan may be started and then
have its speed changed or be stopped by steps that the same Reaction actually
declares. Layer 5 §7 owns this entry rule and eventual program; Layer 3
§4.8.1–4.8.2 owns observed-transition learning. Reuse Layer 0's typed channel
state, Layer 5's existing activation and `ApplyStep` identities, and the
registered environmental episode path. A light or valve Reaction has no
implicit fan-entry rule; another descriptor must declare any analogous
behavior. Counterexamples are an already-running fan at the wrong speed,
an initially off fan subsequently changed from speed 1 to 2, a resident
intervention after Heima's confirmed target, and a post-gap reading with no
known entry state. No new HA-specific control path or causal claim is added.
The earlier one-shot draft lacks declared change/end steps, so
this clarification does not claim that its full program is specified.

The developer approved the resident-experience rule for a temporary extraction
intervention: when its qualifying event finds the fan already operating, Heima
leaves it at its observed speed and admits no speed or stop step for that
intervention, even if the proposed target differs. This does not depend on
whether the earlier fan-on edge can be attributed to a resident, another
automation, or an unknown source; origin evidence remains separate from
authority. For a fan initially off, the same Reaction may start, vary speed,
and stop it through declared steps and ordinary current admission. Its entry
state and later observed transitions belong in typed learning evidence; an
already-active no-op is not a Heima-produced response sample. This is a descriptor-behavior
choice, not a new generic hold or `already_satisfied` rule; a planned target
with an independent purpose, such as a presence-simulation step, retains its
own contract.

**Impact and current boundary.** The earlier Layer-5 §7 draft defined
`environment.air_extraction` as a one-shot `activate_extraction` descriptor,
with `normal_exit_contract=None` and no programmed `off` step. Layer 5 §6.0.1
already skips a call only for an exact, fully matching target, so it does not
by itself protect a running fan at a different speed. Layer 0 §5.1.1/§6
already separates verified origin from target divergence and hold; its rules
remain unchanged. Layer 3 §6.4 supplies the candidate's stable action target,
but not a shutdown program. A versioned descriptor rule must still define
how the running-state test is represented and evaluated before a mismatching
target is admitted, and the required Initial-V3 specialization must declare its
steps and end criterion while preserving the no-command result for a fan
already operating at entry. No new fan-off step, generic Actuator ownership lease, resident-facing
control, or numeric effect is introduced by this record. The V2-to-V3
constraints remain one typed Reaction/ApplyStep path and an Actuator-neutral
runtime; the behavior is declared by the descriptor, not inferred from an
Actuator kind in the common evaluator.

## Whole-bundle lift and baseline decision (partial)

The approved routine output has one record per canonical bundle and context
cell, not one probability per member. Layer 3 §4.4.3 already defines the
trigger/context denominator and the complete/ambiguous outcome rules, but
`P(bundle | subject)` had no sampling frame and `lift` had no formula. Reuse
the same `bundle_assembly_window`, candidate-channel eligibility, coverage
and resident-control join for the subject baseline. The first proposed
UTC-epoch grid was rejected after a counterexample: it split an otherwise
valid bundle at an arbitrary boundary. The approved correction uses the
ratio of matching-bundle start-time exposure to total eligible start-time
exposure across every complete sliding window in the same partition. An
offline interval sweep derives those durations from existing event and
coverage boundaries; it adds no periodic per-device readings. Lift is the
conditional probability divided by the baseline probability; independence
is 1. A zero baseline has no finite lift and cannot pass proposal eligibility.
Overlapping starts are correlated exposure, not independent support; the
temporal-block interval recomputes both probabilities from complete blocks.
A baseline window has no trigger and creates no `ActuationRoutineEpisode`.
This does not change the learner's advisory role, candidate fingerprint or
common Reaction/ApplyStep path.

Counterexamples: a projector trigger with no context still has a meaningful
subject baseline; a rare bundle with no observed baseline match cannot gain
infinite lift; a resident light/valve bundle crossing an arbitrary clock
boundary is still classified by a complete sliding window; an unavailable
fan channel or external command excludes only the affected candidate window;
a crash gap is incomplete coverage, not a no-action sample. The closed keyed
validation-report schema and catalog matrix remain open under CA4-12. These
are textual scenarios; no executable
V3 runtime test has run.

| Scenario | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Complete projector trigger and room history with a cover/fan bundle | One conditional probability and one time-exposure room baseline for the whole bundle; lift uses their ratio. |
| No baseline match, empty cohort or zero-baseline temporal resample | Lift is unavailable and no proposal is eligible; no infinite value enters an artifact. |
| With `W=5 min`, resident bundle actions occur at 20:09 and 20:11 | Complete windows starting from 20:06 inclusive to 20:09 exclusive contain both actions under `(t, t+W]`; the 20:10 clock boundary does not split the pattern. |
| A required channel/trigger stream has incomplete coverage after restart | Starts whose full window crosses the gap are excluded, not counted as no-action negatives. |
| Another automation controls the candidate channel during a window | That window is confounded and excluded, regardless of actuator kind. |
| Two triggers share a time region or a model is retrained | Subject exposure is independent of trigger occurrences and partition boundaries remain fixed; evidence refresh grants no command authority. |
| Many overlapping starts see one resident bundle | They contribute time exposure, not many independent episodes or inflated confidence. |
| A proposal envelope contains two cells, while the artifact has one eligible and one diagnostic record | The proposal is rejected until both proposed cells have exactly one eligible record for the same canonical bundle; no nearest or duplicate record is substituted. |

## Learned program-decision clarification (approved direction; partial)

**Approved decision and gap.** The developer declares legal targets, typed
current-state conditions, step kinds and bounded transitions; Heima estimates
the physical response from complete local observations. A developer need not
declare that a particular later target (for example fan speed 2) is necessary
or assign it a fixed efficacy. The existing exact-class response cells and
`program_evaluator` already carry these responsibilities, but the current
text does not explicitly connect current need, an eligible modification
estimate and the common admission gate. The earlier suggestion to prove a
static list of indispensable future steps is withdrawn.

**Reuse and impact.** Layer 3 §4.8.2 owns the observational meaning and
eligibility of exact-class cells; Layer 5 §3.2 owns the pure descriptor
decision and §7 the extraction example. Their acceptance scenarios, overview,
recap and CA4-12 backlog depend on this clarification. Reuse the existing
`objective_id`, `minimum_practical_delta`, profile objective/intent validation,
finite `transition_class_id`s and current snapshot. Add no candidate field,
generic actuator-specific action branch or numeric performance constant.
V3 retains one descriptor-program and `ApplyStep` path, unlike V2's
light-centered behavior.

**Counterexamples and remaining work.** An active-at-entry fan is never
claimed even if learned data favor a higher target. An initially inactive fan
can change target only if the current condition still calls for it and the
exact required class/context/horizon estimate qualifies; an ineligible cell
does not turn a modification into a no-op success or a static fallback. The
same rule can select a cover or valve target using that descriptor's own
outcome, conditions and bounds. A resident or external physical transition
keeps its origin and is usable only under that profile's cohort/confounder
rules, never relabeled as a Heima action. A stopped or already adequate
condition does not justify an extra step merely because a historic contrast
was favorable. An observed contrast is not proof of causal benefit or of the
whole program's future outcome. Exact extraction matcher, horizon,
objective/intent and per-step mapping remain open CA4-12 work; no V3 runtime
test has run.

| Learned program scenario | Expected contract result |
|---|---|
| Inactive fan started by Heima; current modification condition qualifies and exact speed-change response passes the reviewed threshold | The descriptor may propose that bounded target; Layer 5 still applies current authority, guards, hold and whole-channel admission. |
| The same fan has a favorable historic speed-change cell, but the current modification condition is false | No speed change; the original start trigger need not remain true for an independently declared later step. |
| Fan is active before the activation, even if a favorable speed-change cell exists | No start, modification or terminal command is claimed for that activation. |
| Valve or cover has a qualifying current condition but the exact bounded transition cell is unavailable or below its conservative threshold | That estimate-dependent modification is suppressed; no neighbor class or fixed efficacy substitutes. |
| A resident changes a fan speed or cover position during a complete history window | The physical transition retains resident origin and enters only a profile-approved cohort with full coverage and no excluded confounder. |
| A deterministic terminal step is independently declared and normal exit is currently verified | Evaluate the terminal step through ordinary admission; an unavailable modification estimate alone does not cancel it. |
| Sensor input, actuator state or evidence needed for the current step is unavailable after recovery | Abstain from that step and recalculate from fresh inputs; do not replay a missed modification or credit an unobserved response. |
