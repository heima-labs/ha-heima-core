# CA4-112 — map pan and zoom

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-16).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented`.

## Impact record

- **Existing decisions.** Layer 8 §5.3: the map scrolls inside its own frame, 44×44 targets,
  WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion; §5.1 addresses; §9.1 floor badges; §9.8 tap control; §9.9 editor;
  §9.11 listed pan and zoom on phones as open.
- **Gap.** No zoom, no split between map pan and page scroll, no single-pointer alternative to
  dragging in the editor (WCAG 2.5.7), no rule for the view state.
- **Overlap check.** No new type, field or command. View state is client-only and stays out of
  `HouseMapLayout`, addresses and revisions. No contract toward other layers.
- **Owner.** Layer 8 §9.9, §9.11, §9.13, §16.

## Decisions (developer-approved)

1. Initial view fits the floor's width, never below the scale that keeps 44-pixel device targets.
2. Pinch zooms; one finger pans only beyond the fitted view, otherwise scrolls the page; no
   double-tap zoom.
3. Zoom in, zoom out and fit buttons; arrow and +/− keys; Ctrl/⌘+wheel or trackpad pinch.
4. Range from fitted to 3×; glyph-only device groups below a threshold; upright text.
5. Edit mode: drag on a selected box moves it, on empty space pans; move and size buttons as the
   non-drag alternative.
6. Zoom and pan are client-only; floor switch resets to fitted; a floor badge or room address
   centers the room.
7. Reduced motion: changes jump without animation.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Resident scrolls the Overview on a phone past the map | At the fitted view the swipe scrolls the page. |
| Resident zooms into the living room and pans to the kitchen | Pinch, then one-finger pan inside the frame. |
| Resident taps a light at any zoom | Toggles at once; no double-tap delay. |
| Keyboard-only administrator moves a box | Select, then move and size buttons; no drag needed. |
| Wide floor at 360 px | Opens at the minimum scale and pans; targets stay 44 px. |
| Floor badge to the upper floor | Switches floor and centers the connected room. |
| Two administrators, one zoomed | Zoom is not saved; revisions are unaffected. |

Textual review only; no V3 runtime test has run.
