# CA4-99 — execution reference per job basis

**Status:** specification reviewed (2026-10-14).
**Evidence type:** `textual_contract_and_scenario_review`.
**Runtime test:** `not_run_v3_not_implemented` (the contradiction was reproduced in the
implementation worktree with the real cycle clock, scheduler and a fake timer; that is not V3
runtime evidence for this specification).

## Impact record

- **Approved decisions.** CA4-20: `wall_clock` deadlines follow corrected wall-clock time;
  `elapsed` countdowns are never changed by a civil correction; cycle instants strictly increase
  (`max(wall, previous + 1 µs)`); re-arm by `job_id` replacement with an unchanged canonical due.
- **Contradiction.** §3.1 computed every countdown against the cycle-instant clock, which §3.5
  holds above a regressed wall clock, so a `wall_clock` job fired before its civil due.
- **Counterexample for the opposite fix.** Using the wall reading for every job lengthens an
  `elapsed` countdown under a frozen floor (a 30 s hold armed at 100.000001 with wall 98 would get
  32 s). Two references, one per basis, are needed.
- **Owner/dependents.** Layer 0 §3.1 (owner), §3.5, §13. Layer 2 §3.2 (`max_source_age` expiry)
  and Layer 5 §6.0 (next-step slot) only declare a basis and need no change.

## Rule

- `wall_clock`: countdown = `due − current wall-clock reading (UTC, unfloored)`, at arm and at
  every re-arm (detection at a cycle start, at the periodic check or at bootstrap). A due at or
  before that reading fires once, immediately.
- `elapsed`: countdown = `due − cycle-instant clock`, where the cycle-instant clock is
  `max(wall reading, last cycle instant + 1 µs)` at that reading (§3.5).
- The cycle instant is history time only. A cycle requested by a firing job gets the floored
  instant; a due never becomes a history instant.
- `CronEntry` computes "now" with the reference of its basis.

## Scenario review

| Scenario | Result |
|---|---|
| Wall 100 → 98 at monotonic 1, `wall_clock` due 110 | Re-armed to 12 s; fires at wall 110. |
| Same correction found by the periodic check | Re-armed against the check's wall reading; same result. |
| Bootstrap with wall 1.5 s below the durable floor, `WeeklyRecurrence` due ahead | Armed against the wall reading. |
| `elapsed` 30 s hold armed at cycle 100.000001 (wall 98) | 30 s countdown. |
| `wall_clock` due already passed after a forward jump | Fires once, immediately. |
| Re-arm of a pending job | Same `job_id`, unchanged due, no duplicate delivery. |
